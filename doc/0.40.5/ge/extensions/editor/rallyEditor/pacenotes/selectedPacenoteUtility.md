Small blue floating "Selected Pacenote Utility" window of the rally editor. For the selected pacenote, it displays three blocks: "Actual" (corner call, length and shape actually generated by the composer), "Adjustment" (intensity risk plus/minus, longer/shorter corner, ignored length) and "Metadata" (calibration metadata: descriptor or intensity, direction, length, shape). It reads the first corner (slot 4) of the pacenote's structure.

## Notes

- The window is not displayed if no pacenote is selected, if it is missing (pn.missing) or if the camera path is playing.
- Displays "<none>" when a piece of information is absent.
